WindGet Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026 · App: WindGet (com.windget.app) · A Sweathkumar project

WindGet is a widget app for Android. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send your personal data to us. This policy explains what the app reads, why, and where that information goes.

Information that stays on your phone

The following is read and stored only on your device to show it in widgets. It is never uploaded by WindGet.

  • Things you enter: tasks, notes, calendar marks, water count, countdown events, timers and settings.
  • Device status: battery level and health, storage and memory use, network type and data used since restart, ringer mode and volume, device name and Android version, and your next alarm time.
  • Calendar events (only if you allow Calendar access): titles and times of upcoming events, for the “Up next” and “Next event” widgets.
  • App usage (only if you allow Usage access): today’s screen time and most-used apps, for the “Screen time” widget.

Android may include this local data in your device backup if you have backup turned on; that is managed by Google under your Google account settings.

Information sent to a third party

To show weather, WindGet sends the coordinates of the city you choose — or, only if you choose “use my location” and allow Location access, your approximate location — to Open-Meteo, a free weather service, over an encrypted connection. A city name you search for is sent to Open-Meteo’s geocoding service. No identifier, account or other personal data is sent with these requests. Open-Meteo’s own terms and privacy policy apply to those requests.

Permissions

  • Internet, network state, Wi-Fi state: weather and the WiFi / Network widgets.
  • Approximate location (optional): weather for where you are.
  • Calendar (optional): the “Up next” and “Next event” widgets.
  • Usage access (optional, granted in system settings): the “Screen time” widget.
  • Notifications, alarms & reminders, run at startup, vibrate: focus and habit timer alerts.

You can turn any of these off at any time in Android Settings; the related widget simply stops showing that data.

Children

WindGet is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Open WindGet → Settings → Clear all data, or uninstall the app. Because nothing is stored on our servers, there is nothing else to delete.
